GTA 6 Blackout

Hide the hype. Keep your money.

A Chrome extension that hides GTA 6 videos, posts, searches and news across the web, so the hype doesn't talk you into buying it.

Blocks pages before they load

GTA 6 searches, articles, store pages and subreddits go to a calm block screen instead.

Hides it everywhere else

YouTube, Reddit, X, search results, TikTok and shops: GTA 6 items disappear as the page loads.

Catches indirect references

"Rockstar's next game", Jason and Lucia, Leonida, "GTA trailer" and the name in other languages.

Built to resist the impulse

No off switch in the popup. Strict mode blocks all of GTA and Rockstar, and takes an hour to turn off.

Private by design

Everything happens in your browser. No account, no analytics, no data collected.
