GTA 6 Blackout privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: GTA 6 Blackout collects no data. Nothing you browse leaves your device.

What the extension does with page content

To hide GTA 6 content, the extension reads the text, link addresses and image descriptions of the pages you visit, and the address of each page. This happens entirely inside your browser. The content is compared against a fixed list of GTA 6 patterns and then discarded. It is never stored, logged or transmitted.

What is stored, and where

The following is stored with Chrome's extension storage, on your device only:

  • A count of items hidden and pages blocked
  • The date the extension was installed, for the "days strong" counter
  • Whether Strict mode is on, and when it's scheduled to turn off
  • The number of items hidden in each open tab, kept only until the browser closes

None of this is synced to your Google account or sent anywhere. Removing the extension deletes it.

What is not collected

No personal information, browsing history, account details, analytics, crash reports or identifiers of any kind. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no third-party code, trackers or ads.

Sharing

Because no data is collected, none is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Changes
